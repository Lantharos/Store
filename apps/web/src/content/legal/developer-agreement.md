Version 2026-11. You accept this agreement in the dashboard before your first submission, and we record when. If it changes, we ask you to accept the new version before your next release.

## Your app

- You own the app you submit, or have the right to distribute it, including everything bundled with it.
- The licence you declare is the real licence of the app and of what it bundles. At launch, apps on Schelf are open source.
- The listing is honest: the name, description, screenshots and permissions describe what people will get.
- The app follows the [review guidelines](/docs/review-guidelines).

## Your account

- Keep your GitHub account, repository and dashboard tokens safe. Turn on two-factor sign-in for GitHub.
- If any of them is compromised, tell us at [legal@schelf.store](mailto:legal@schelf.store) within 72 hours of finding out, and remove the trusted publisher and tokens in the dashboard.
- You're responsible for releases published through your trusted publishers and tokens.

## What you let Schelf do

So that Schelf can deliver your app, you allow us to:

- store, copy, mirror and serve the files you publish, and the listing that goes with them;
- unpack, scan and inspect them, and show reviewers the source changes between releases;
- sign each approved release with the store's key and publish it in the catalog and update feeds;
- show your name, app and listing on the website and in the Schelf app, and in links and previews of them;
- delist or remove an app or release, and stop it from starting on people's computers when it's harmful, with a statement of the reasons sent to you.

## What Schelf doesn't promise

We aim to review quickly and keep the service running, but we don't guarantee that an app will be accepted, how long review takes, or that the service is always available. Schelf is provided as it is. As far as the law allows, we're not liable for lost income or indirect damage, and our total liability to you is limited to €100.

## Ending it

You can delete your account at any time; your apps are delisted, and people who installed them keep them. We can end this agreement if you break it in a serious way, with the reasons in writing and a chance to reply.

## Law

This agreement is governed by the law of the operator's country of residence, without taking away protections you have where you live.
