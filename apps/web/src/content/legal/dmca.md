title: Copyright
description: How to tell us an app uses your work without permission, and how developers can respond.
---

If an app on Schelf uses your code, name, logo or other work without permission, tell us and we'll look at it.

## Sending a notice

Use **Report a problem** on the app's page and choose “It uses my work”, or write to [legal@schelf.store](mailto:legal@schelf.store). Include:

1. Your name, address, phone number and email.
2. The work you own, and where we can see the original.
3. The app on Schelf, and what in it uses your work.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't allowed by you, your agent or the law.
5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that your notice is accurate and that you own the work or may act for its owner.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

We forward the notice to the developer, delist the app or release while it's being sorted out when the notice is complete, and tell you what we did.

## Counter-notices

If you're the developer and you believe the notice is wrong, reply to our email with your name, address and phone number, the app that was removed, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe it was removed by mistake, your consent to the jurisdiction of a court where you live, and your signature. We send it to the person who sent the notice. Unless they tell us within 10 to 14 business days that they've gone to court, we put the app back.

## Repeat infringement

We close the developer accounts of people who repeatedly publish other people's work.
