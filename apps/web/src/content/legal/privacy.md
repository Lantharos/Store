Last changed on 12 November 2026. Questions go to [privacy@schelf.store](mailto:privacy@schelf.store).

## If you install apps

You don't need an account to download or install apps, and Schelf doesn't track you.

- **Downloads and updates** are counted per app, version and kind of system (such as Linux on Arm), and whether it was a new install or an update. Nothing that identifies your device, no address and nothing about you is stored with those counts. We only see totals per day.
- **The Schelf app** never sends us the list of apps you have. It downloads the public catalog and checks for updates on its own.
- **Rollouts** are decided on your computer from a random number that stays on your computer.
- **Server logs** with IP addresses are kept by our hosting provider for up to 7 days to keep the service running and stop abuse, then deleted.

The website doesn't use cookies unless you sign in as a developer, and has no analytics or advertising.

## If you report something

We keep what you write in the report form, your name and email if you give them, and what we decided. We use them to handle the report and to tell you the outcome, and keep them as long as the law requires us to be able to show how we handled it.

## If you're a developer

We keep your account: your name, email, profile picture if you upload one, sign-in methods (a GitHub account, passkeys or email codes), the namespaces you've proven, your apps, releases and their review history, and when you accepted the developer agreement. We use them to run your account and to review and publish your apps. Your developer name and your apps are public. Your email isn't.

If you connect GitHub, we show your GitHub profile picture in the dashboard, and on your personal publisher page until you add a logo. We fetch it from GitHub and serve it from schelf.store, so visitors' browsers never contact GitHub for it. You can upload your own picture instead.

You can delete your account from the dashboard at any time. Your apps are delisted, and your personal details are deleted within 30 days, except what we must keep to show how we handled reports and reviews.

## Who helps us

| Service    | What for                                                                              |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cloudflare | Hosting, storage, email and download counting                                         |
| GitHub     | Signing in with GitHub, your profile picture, and the build records of each release   |
| OpenRouter | A first pass over releases before a person reviews them; it sees the release, not you |

## Your rights

You can ask us what we have about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to move it elsewhere, by writing to [privacy@schelf.store](mailto:privacy@schelf.store). You can also complain to your data protection authority.
