## Who we are

Schelf is run by Lantharos. You can reach us at [legal@schelf.store](mailto:legal@schelf.store).

## Apps are made by their developers

Every app and extension on Schelf is made by its developer, who licenses it to you under the licence shown on its page. Schelf doesn't make, own or license the apps it lists. If you have a problem with how an app works, the developer's support link is on its page.

What Schelf does is check each release before it's listed, deliver it, and keep track of it afterwards. A person reviews every release, but review can miss things, and no app comes with a guarantee from us.

## Keeping you safe

We can remove an app or a release from Schelf at any time, for example when it turns out to be harmful, breaks the law, or its developer asks us to. When an app or release is dangerous, we can also stop it from starting on computers where it's installed. Your files stay where they are, and the app tells you why it was stopped and offers to remove it. We use this only to protect people, never to settle disagreements with developers.

## Reports

Every app page has a **Report a problem** link. Use it to tell us about malware, illegal content, copyright problems or security issues. A person reads every report. If you leave an email, we confirm we got it and tell you what we decided and why. If you're a developer and we act on a report about your app, you get a statement of what we did and why, and you can reply to have a person look at it again.

Automatic checks only stop obvious problems before review, like files that don't match. Every other decision to reject, remove or stop an app is made by a person.

## What you agree not to do

Don't use Schelf to attack, overload or scrape the service, to get around a block on an app, or to pass off something as an app that it isn't.
