## Contact

Write to [legal@schelf.store](mailto:legal@schelf.store) for anything about Schelf: questions, legal notices, requests from authorities, problems with your data, and disputes about app names. We answer in English.

Some things have an address of their own, and reach the right person sooner there:

- [dmca@schelf.store](mailto:dmca@schelf.store) for copyright notices
- [abuse@schelf.store](mailto:abuse@schelf.store) for malware, scams and other abuse
- [security@schelf.store](mailto:security@schelf.store) for security problems in Schelf itself
- [privacy@schelf.store](mailto:privacy@schelf.store) for your data
- [support@schelf.store](mailto:support@schelf.store) for help with publishing

To report a problem with a specific app, use the **Report a problem** link on its page. It reaches the same people and tells us exactly which app you mean.
