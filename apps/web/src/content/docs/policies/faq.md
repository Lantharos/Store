title: Questions
description: Short answers to the things developers ask most.
---

## Does it cost anything?

No. Publishing is free, and so is hosting your downloads. Paid apps will come later.

## Does my repository have to be public?

No. Private repositories publish the same way. Reviewers look at the build instead of the source diff, and your source stays private.

## Can I publish closed-source apps?

Not yet. At launch every app on Schelf is open source under an OSI-approved or FSF-free licence.

## Do I need a Sabine app?

No. You can publish an AppImage you build however you like, or a Luft extension. Sabine apps get the most out of Schelf: one build for every system, and updates on Windows and macOS.

## How long does review take?

Usually within a day for a first release, and faster for updates. Updates that widen permissions take longer.

## What do you know about the people who install my app?

How many installs and updates each version gets per day and per system. Nothing else: no addresses, devices or accounts, because Schelf doesn't keep any.

## Can I keep my app on Flathub too?

Yes. Schelf shows each app once: if yours is on both, people on Luft get the Schelf listing.

## I don't use GitHub.

Publish from any CI or your own computer with a token and [`schelf publish`](/docs/cli). Those releases are marked “uploaded by the developer”.

## How do I remove my app?

Write to [legal@schelf.store](mailto:legal@schelf.store) and we'll delist it. It disappears from the store, and people who have it keep it. Deleting your account delists all your apps.
